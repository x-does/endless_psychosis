# Character forms: visual memory, not mechanical stacking

Mi and Elite approved three independently clamped run-local axes: **Radiance, Echo, Bone**. Choices add once, pickups add small traces, and each axis decays `.008` per active second. Pausing freezes decay. Effect expiry does not erase residue; nothing is written into permanent saves.

Damage contributes `.025 Echo`, at most four times per rolling 12 seconds. Breath adds `.04 Radiance/−.03 Echo`; heart adds `.04 Radiance/−.03 Bone`; shield adds `.03 Bone`. Techniques add `.06 Radiance`; a boss win adds `.10 Radiance/−.05 Echo`. Sparks add no visual residue.

A dominant form enters at `.60` with a `.15` lead over the other axes; it remains until below `.45` or displaced by another qualifying axis. This hysteresis avoids flicker. All layers keep rendering: light-core and tether, ghost veil, ivory ribs/skull. Non-dominant mixtures remain visible rather than normalizing into one color.

The human fill fades by at most 65%. A stable collision-envelope outline and foot markers disclose unchanged physics, including when sliding or jumping. Reduced motion suppresses orbit/trailing motion. No appearance changes damage, speed, invulnerability or collision dimensions. Forms are symbolic experiences, not labels of illness, virtue or worth.

Active benefits add a separate signature: grounding foot ring, compassion crescent, connected cyan triangle, closed Bottle shell, Push chevrons, Tune veil. During aftermath the signature is broken/dashed, and the HUD labels the phase and its remaining duration separately.
