# Endless Psychosis: identity redesign

## Correction to the first portfolio

The owner rejected Night Orchard, Borrowed Gravity, and Midnight Switchboard: separate mini-games with a shared label did not embody Endless Psychosis. The replacement changes the simulations, objectives, failure conditions, and visual direction, not only the names.

## Council decision

Both primary design members support three interpretations of repetition as a threat:

| Game | Question | Mechanic that earns the identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loop House | Where can I stand? | Your earlier positions return as echoes in the opposite room layer. Exits lead to another altered version of the house. |
| False Orbit | What will catch me? | Real anchors coexist with uncapturable afterimages; previous routes leave interference. No final delivery. |
| Dead Frequency | Has this already happened? | Accepted traffic feeds a replay pool. Correct-looking messages can be stale; the ledger, not the message’s claims, decides. |

Shared direction: coal, stained ivory, aged amber/acid accents, industrial instruments, architectural plans, misregistered silhouettes. Atmosphere may be unreliable; hitboxes, evidence, and controls remain reliable. No medical simulation or diagnosis-based enemies.

## Practical choices

- Two deliberate games and one real-time timing game keep distinct rhythms.
- Loop House previews the delayed echo before a turn is committed; room templates must have verified safe solutions.
- False Orbit distinguishes anchor types by persistent geometry; reveal emphasizes existing cues. Tap versus hold must not produce accidental launches. Launch windows are measured.
- Dead Frequency uses operator beats, four incoming slots, two channels, and an upstream buffer. An original has an intact seal and a sequence above its route ledger. Acceptance reserves the sequence immediately. Reading is free; transmit, discard, verify, and advance spend a beat. Four integrity errors end a run. Original delivery scores 10 and erased echoes score 5. An evidence-following operator can sustain the generator indefinitely.
- Difficulty is bounded. The games continue until failure rather than turning mathematically impossible.
- New routes: `/games/loop-house`, `/games/false-orbit`, `/games/dead-frequency`. Previous prototype URLs are aliases to preserve links. `/original` retains the arena. Existing production saves remain isolated.

## Advisory findings

The advisory member correctly identified the need for telegraphed echoes, persistent phantom cues, legible mobile evidence, and fair generators. Suggestions that conflict with the brief or primary designs were not adopted: finite six-leg progression, sudden unavoidable channel-overflow loss, hidden/moving physics, and route deletion. Its assertions about old documentation or accessibility are not treated as verified evidence.

## Original research provenance

The earlier research explored twelve touch-first concepts. Relevant precedents included [Hoplite](https://www.magmafortress.com/p/hoplite.html), [Mini Metro](https://dinopoloclub.com/games/mini-metro/), [Super Hexagon](https://superhexagon.com/), and [Gorogoa](https://www.annapurnainteractive.com/en/games/gorogoa). These establish genre precedents, not demand for this trilogy. [Pointer Events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Pointer_events) support unified browser input; [Capacitor](https://capacitorjs.com/docs) is a possible future packaging route. Native packaging is outside this browser release.
